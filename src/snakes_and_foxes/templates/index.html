Opening setup

White and Black begin in the hub. The outer ring is fully occupied, alternating fox then snake around all sixteen spokes.

Rolling

Roll all six dice each turn. Black pips give your piece moves, red triangles activate foxes, and green squiggles activate snakes. On the opening throw, reroll until at least one black pip appears.

Movement

Spend every move exactly. You may reverse along spokes, but ring movement must still follow that ring's allowed direction.

Ring directions

Red rings: counter-clockwise Green rings: clockwise Blue outer ring: both ways

Enemy order

After the active piece finishes moving, foxes resolve first and snakes resolve second. Each activated enemy moves one step toward the active piece.
