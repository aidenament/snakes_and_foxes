    The ancient board waits

    Guide one piece to the rim and back again.

    Sun and Moon alternate turns. Foxes move first, snakes move second, and both hunt only the active piece.

    How the board works

    • Black pips grant one move each.
    • From the center you may step to any node on the first ring.
    • Every other ring allows one forward move, one inward move, and one outward move.
    • Either surviving piece wins by touching the outer ring and returning to center later.
    • Foxes and snakes chase only the active piece using board distance.
